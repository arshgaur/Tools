Why Compress Your Images?

Image compression is essential for:

  • Faster website loading times (improves SEO ranking)
  • Reduced bandwidth usage and server costs
  • Better user experience on mobile devices
  • Easier sharing via email or social media
  • Optimized storage space on your devices

Best Practices for Image Compression

For optimal results:

  • Use WebP format for the best quality/size ratio (supported by all modern browsers)
  • Aim for 70-85% quality for JPEG images
  • Use PNG only for images with transparency
  • Resize large images before compression (our tool maintains aspect ratio)
