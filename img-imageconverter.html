About Image Conversion

Our free online image converter allows you to change your image files between different formats while maintaining quality. Here's when to use each format:

Image Format Guide

  • JPEG (JPG): Best for photographs and realistic images with many colors
  • PNG: Ideal for images with transparency or sharp edges like logos
  • WebP: Modern format offering superior compression (recommended for web)
  • GIF: Suitable for simple animations and low-color images
  • BMP: Uncompressed format, not recommended for web use
  • TIFF: High quality format often used in professional photography

How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload your image by dragging & dropping or clicking "Browse Files"
  2. Select your desired output format from the dropdown
  3. Adjust the quality slider if needed (higher = better quality but larger file)
  4. Click "Convert Image" and wait for processing
  5. Download your converted image
